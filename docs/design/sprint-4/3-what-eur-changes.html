What EUR changes

  • Prices are converted to euros every day, at the ECB reference rate.
  • Cash earns the euro overnight rate (€STR), not the US rate.
  • Nothing is hedged: a US ticker still moves with the dollar.

What EUR changes

  • Prices are converted to euros every day, at the ECB reference rate.
  • Cash earns the euro overnight rate (€STR), not the US rate.
  • US dollar bond funds (IEF, LQD, HYG, EMB, TIP) are hedged to the euro. Shares, gold and European or global bond funds are not.

The hedge is an estimate from the rate gap, not a fund's own hedge.
