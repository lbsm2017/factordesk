Return is in percentage points (pp) of the starting value, and the lines add up to the portfolio's total return. Risk share is each line's part of the portfolio's total risk; the shares add up to 100%. Target is the weight you set, end is the weight at the end of the period.

How this is computed

Each holding's return is linked through the rebalances, so the contributions add up exactly to the total return. Group rows are the sums of their holdings. Cash and cost are shown on their own rows and are not part of any asset class.

Risk share splits the portfolio's volatility by holding, using each holding's weight and how it moves with the rest of the portfolio.
